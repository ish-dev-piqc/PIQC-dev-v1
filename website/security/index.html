Our approach

Three ideas shape how PIQC is built.

Built from the protocol itself

PIQC's intelligence comes from the protocol itself — requirements, schedules, definitions, and source text. That is the material the platform is designed to work from.

Controlled, role-based access

Access is controlled and role-based. People work in the surfaces their role needs — a coordinator's view, an auditor's workspace, a sponsor's deliverables — rather than one shared everything.

Actions are traceable

Review actions — accept, edit, reject, flag — are recorded with the reviewer and time, so the work can be revisited and understood later. Traceability is a design principle, not an afterthought.

The draft-aid boundary

PIQC advises and drafts; humans review, decide, and approve.

Nothing PIQC produces is final on its own. Extractions, checklists, findings, and deliverables are drafts — surfaced for a named person to review, change, or reject. That boundary is deliberate, and it holds everywhere in the product.

AI drafts. Humans decide. Everything is traceable.
