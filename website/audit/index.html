Live today · Findings

Findings that are risk-scored and traceable — end to end.

Every finding traces the same chain: the protocol requirement, the vendor responsibility it maps to, and the evidence reviewed. Findings are scored across two impact surfaces — data integrity and patient safety.

  • Severity — Critical, Major, Minor, or Observation — is assigned by the auditor, never proposed autonomously by the AI.
  • Each finding links back to its protocol source with section and page.
  • Evidence attachments stay connected to the finding they support.

AI drafts. Humans decide. Everything is traceable.

Live today · Issue → CAPA

From finding to Issue to CAPA — without re-typing the context.

Raise an Issue from a finding and the CAPA arrives pre-filled from the finding’s context — requirement, responsibility, and evidence carried forward instead of transcribed. The issue’s state follows its CAPA, so there is one lifecycle to track, not two.

  1. Draft PIQC pre-fills the CAPA from the finding’s requirement, responsibility, and evidence. The auditor edits every field.
  2. Needs revision Review feedback sends the CAPA back with its history intact — nothing is lost between rounds.
  3. Accepted The strongest in-app state: ready to export. Formal approval happens in your QMS, after export — deliberately outside PIQC.

PIQC deliberately stops at Accepted — it never marks a CAPA “approved.” Approval belongs to your quality system, and the boundary is built in.

Live today · Report drafting

AI drafts the report. Two human confirmations before a word lands.

PIQC drafts the executive summary and conclusions from the findings the auditor logged. But drafting is not writing: nothing enters the report until the auditor gives two explicit confirmations — first reviewing the draft text, then confirming the write-back itself.

  • Earned write-back: AI text reaches the report only after both confirmations.
  • Every accepted passage is attributed as drafted, with the auditor’s confirmation logged.
  • Decline either step and the draft stays a draft — the report is untouched.

Humans approve every word that reaches the report.

Live today · Amendments & lineage

When the protocol changes, the audit knows.

Because Audit Mode runs on the same parsed protocol as everything else, a protocol amendment surfaces alerts on the audit objects it touches — for the auditor to adopt or dismiss, never applied silently.

Amendment alerts

Version changes flag affected extractions and the audit items built on them. Each alert is an adopt-or-dismiss decision that belongs to the auditor.

Lineage on every object

Findings, Issues, and CAPAs carry per-object history — who changed what, and when — so any conclusion can be followed back to its origin.

Evidence, attached in place

Evidence attachments live on the object they support. The chain from protocol requirement to vendor responsibility to evidence stays intact through export.
